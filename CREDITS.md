# Credits

## Course material
This project is a study companion for the NPTEL course **Software Project Management** (noc26_cs152).

- **Instructors:** Prof. Rajib Mall (IIT Kharagpur) and Prof. Durga Prasad Mohapatra (NIT Rourkela)
- **Platform:** NPTEL (National Programme on Technology Enhanced Learning), an initiative of the IITs and IISc, Government of India, https://nptel.ac.in
- **Reference book named in the course:** B. Hughes, M. Cotterell, R. Mall, *Software Project Management*, 6th edition, McGraw Hill (not included here)

The assignment questions (2026 and 2019 runs) and the NPTEL AI mock-paper questions in `src/qbank.js`, and the lecture facts in the topic pages, come from the course assignments, slides, summary notes and lecture transcripts. All of that material belongs to NPTEL, IIT Kharagpur and the instructors. The explanations, labs, generators, case studies and analysis are original work. This project is **not affiliated with or endorsed by NPTEL or IIT Kharagpur**. If the rights holders want any material removed, please open an issue and it will be removed promptly.
